Open your workspace

  1. Link your WhatsApp

    When prompted, scan the terminal QR code using WhatsApp’s Linked Devices. Sessions persist between restarts.

  2. Choose who gets a reply

    Add a number with its country code, set a fixed reply, and enable reply access.

  3. Set your assistant’s boundaries

    Configure AI instructions, reply delay, and per-chat cooldown. Saved changes apply to subsequent requests.

Local access.
Clear boundaries.

The workspace is served only on your computer. Credentials and session files are not exposed by the web server.

AI generation uses your configured model provider. “Local workspace” does not mean AI processing happens offline.

This project uses WhatsApp Web automation, not the official WhatsApp Business Platform. Use it responsibly and follow WhatsApp’s terms. Ace is not affiliated with WhatsApp or Meta.
